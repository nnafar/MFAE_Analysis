Experiment folder naming (required by the bulk analysis)
========================================================

Name each experiment's data folder (and its experiment_id in the config) as:

    YYMMDD_CellType_Treatment_Chip_Experiment-<Pressure>Pa-<Voltage>V-<Duration><unit>-frame<PulseFrame>

  * exactly five underscore-separated fields before the first '-'
    (no extra '_' or '-' inside a field)
  * whole numbers only; unit is ms, us or s
  * Pa, V and frame are literal text

Electroporated cells:

    260128_MDAMB231_WT_Chip1_Experiment3-1100Pa-100V-5ms-frame10

Aspiration only (no pulse): use 0V, 0ms and frame0:

    260326_MDAMB231_WT_Chip2_Experiment283-1100Pa-0V-0ms-frame0

Folders that don't match are skipped by Bulk_Analysis. Check a name with:

    python MFA_analysis.py my_config.yaml --check
